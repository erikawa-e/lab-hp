Our plasmon paper is now published in Nature Communications! We demonstrate strong coupling between the collective charge oscillation (plasmon) of electrons floating on liquid helium and a radio-frequency photon confined in a compact LC resonator circuit — with a coupling rate exceeding the loss rates of both systems. Time-resolved measurements directly show coherent, oscillatory energy exchange between the plasmon and the photon, and the same circuit doubles as a sensitive probe of the electron system, revealing the Wigner crystal transition and ripplon-limited plasmon decay.

**[Strong Coupling Between RF Photons and Plasmons of Electrons on Liquid Helium](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-77527-8)**
Asher Jennings\*, Ivan Grytsenko, Thomas Giovansili, Itay Josef Barabash, Oleksiy Rybalko, Yiran Tian, Jun Wang, Hiroki Ikegami, and Erika Kawakami\*
*Nature Communications* (2026)
