The [International Workshop on Quantum Dot Technology (InQuot2026)](https://sites.google.com/view/masquot-en/workshops/inquot2026), organized by masQuot/InGeQT under the ASPIRE UK program and co-hosted by us, was held at RIKEN Wako Campus, with about 100 participants. Our own Asher Jennings served as the overall moderator. Thank you to all the speakers and participants for making it such a great event!

From our group, Yiran Tian received the InQuot2026 Best Presenter Award and Ivan Grytsenko received the masQuot 2026 Outstanding Presenter Award — congratulations to both! The awards were presented by Prof. Tetsuo Kodera (Institute of Science Tokyo), the Japan-side PI of ASPIRE UK.
